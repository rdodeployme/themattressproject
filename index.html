Why it matters

A bed is a basic. Not everyone has one.

Sleep is where kids grow, learn and recover. Families can't start again without somewhere clean and safe to rest.

Photo to come — mum and twin girls with their new mattresses (faces not identifiable)
Starting again

A mum and her seven-year-old twin girls, rebuilding after family violence.

When families leave violence, they often leave with very little. A clean, comfortable bed of their own helps a new place feel like home.

Photo to come — bed springs covered in fertiliser bags
This is Australia

First Nations kids sleeping on bare springs, covered with fertiliser bags.

No child should go to bed like this. A proper mattress and clean bedding is a basic, not a luxury.
